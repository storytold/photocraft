PhotoCraft PSD document icon (photocraft-document.svg and photocraft-document.ico).
SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT OR Apache-2.0

The composition is adapted from design 01, "red outline page", one of the icon
redesigns JustCallMeAD posted in
https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft/issues/308, redrawn and finished for PhotoCraft.
The folded corner is filled into a rounded flap, as on the shipped PdfCraft document
icon, which is where that part of the treatment comes from.

The lettering is outlined type (Geist, SIL Open Font License 1.1), converted to
curves before export; the artwork embeds no font, no live text and no external
image. Regenerate the ICO with `cargo run -p xtask -- ico`; the artwork and its
provenance are recorded in ATTRIBUTION.md.
