# PhotoCraft Splash Screen Artwork

The official startup splash screen artwork for PhotoCraft, featuring the nine-tailed kitsune with brow diamond, cosmic constellations, gold filigree, swirling paint waves, and artisan tools.

## Specifications

- **Format:** JPEG (1024 × 571, 72 DPI, sRGB)
- **Aspect Ratio:** ~16:9 landscape (~1.79:1)
- **Palette:** Deep indigo/nebula blue, gold filigree, vibrant paint tones (cyan, turquoise, magenta, sunset orange)
- **License:** MIT OR Apache-2.0
