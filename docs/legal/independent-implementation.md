# Independent implementation and provenance

LumaFoundry targets functional editing capabilities and file interoperability through
independently written code. Similar results or familiar workflows are not permission to
copy another product's source, artwork, wording or distinctive visual presentation.
This policy governs new work and takes precedence over historical instructions to reproduce
another application's appearance. It is an engineering policy, not a legal clearance opinion.

## Implementation boundaries

- Use public specifications, published research, independently recorded functional observations
  and appropriately licensed open-source implementations. Record the source and relevant licence.
  Public availability alone does not grant permission to copy code or explanatory text.
- Do not use leaked source, decompiled code, extracted shaders, executable resources, proprietary
  SDK material without a suitable licence, or code reconstructed from such material. Do not use
  an AI tool to translate, paraphrase or regenerate prohibited source or assets.
- Write our own UI copy and documentation. Use LumaFoundry names for new tools and retain an
  independent graphic identity. Review familiar menu arrangements, icons and overall presentation
  for more than just individually functional elements; renaming is not a substitute for review.
- Do not trace product screenshots, embed screenshot fragments in controls, or extract icons,
  cursors, fonts, brushes, patterns, gradients, profiles, presets, stock artwork or model weights
  from another application's installation. An individually permissive licence is an exception
  only for the specific material it covers, with its conditions fulfilled and recorded.
- Public file specifications guide compatibility; specification text, sample code and bundled
  examples can have separate terms. Preserve existing machine identifiers and format fields
  when interoperability needs them, without implying endorsement.
- Every third-party asset needs its author, source, licence, local licence text and attribution
  in ATTRIBUTION.md. Record modifications. Unknown provenance is unresolved, not assumed original.
  Preserve upstream copyright and licence notices on legitimate open-source contributions.
- For AI features, record each model's source, exact version/hash and licence separately from
  the runtime's licence. Check intended distribution and use. Reference images and generated
  outputs need their own rights assessment; local inference does not establish ownership or
  guarantee non-infringement. Do not advertise a blanket legal-safety guarantee.
- Test fixtures must be original, public domain or licensed for the intended test use. Separate
  document ownership from the software used to author it. Keep external corpora out of packages;
  a fixture's redistribution licence does not automatically apply to embedded fonts or artwork.

## Release review

Before publishing an installer, website build or demo bundle, resolve provenance uncertainties
for what actually ships. Verify asset and dependency licences, required notices, optional fonts,
model distribution terms, screenshots and test-data exclusions. Review the inherited interface's
combined presentation and marketing independently of feature-parity tests. Where rights or terms
remain uncertain, obtain qualified legal review or replace/omit that material; do not mark this
review complete based on a keyword scan or passing tests. Record the reviewed revision and
artifact inventory. Copyright, trademark, patents, contract terms and regional law are distinct
questions; this document certifies none of them.
