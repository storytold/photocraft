Original contextual task bar demonstration assets

Files: contextual-taskbar-demo.ppm, contextual-taskbar-before.png,
       contextual-taskbar-after.png
Copyright (c) 2026 John Pantrakis

The PPM is original synthetic RGB artwork, generated without any reference photo:
200 by 160 pixels; a red disc centred at (100,80), radius 45, on a blue field.
The repeating noise is (13*x + 7*y) modulo 17. RGB is (210,50,30)+noise
inside the disc and (60,100,170)+noise outside it. The PNGs are unmodified
PhotoCraft offscreen captures made with the snapshot example on 2026-10-10.
No personal photo or proprietary editor asset was used. Existing UI elements
retain their upstream attributions in ATTRIBUTION.md.
