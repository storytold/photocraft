The **Layers** panel can show a compact list of layer names or larger previews. These are display settings: changing them does not change the image, layer contents, or document history.

## Hide or resize layer thumbnails

1. Open the **Layers** panel using **Window › Layers** if it is not visible.
2. Open the panel menu (**☰** in the Layers panel header), then choose **Panel Options…**.
3. Under **Thumbnail Size**, select **None** to hide the previews, or **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large** to show them at the desired size.
4. Click **OK**.

**None** also makes layer rows more compact. To restore thumbnails, open **Panel Options…** again and choose a size. The choice is saved as a panel preference, not as a change to the document.

## Choose what a thumbnail shows

In the Layers panel menu, open **Panel Options…**. The **Thumbnail Contents** section offers two choices:

- **Layer Bounds** frames the content of each layer, which can make small objects easier to recognize.
- **Entire Document** shows the full canvas area in each preview, useful for seeing where a layer sits in the composition.

Click **OK** to apply the change. You can also toggle **Clip Thumbnails to Layer Bounds** directly from the Layers panel menu.

These options affect only the previews in the Layers panel. They do not crop, scale, or otherwise modify any layers.
